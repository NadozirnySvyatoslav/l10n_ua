Ukraine - eCommerce Documents

After a successful online payment, the Print button of the order confirmation page gives the customer the Ukrainian primary document — акт or видаткова накладна — of the posted invoice, instead of the Odoo order PDF.

  • Installs by itself next to website_sale and l10n_ua_sale_documents.
  • The document follows the company setting of l10n_ua_sale_documents: акт, видаткова накладна, or chosen by the invoice lines.
  • Until the paid order has a posted invoice (for instance after a test payment), the Рахунок-фактура is printed.
  • For Odoo 19 Community.
