Ukraine - Sale Documents in E-mails

Ukrainian invoice, service act and delivery note in the e-mails of Sales and Invoicing for Odoo 19

About

Closes the document flow of an online shop or a service company in Ukraine: order → payment → posted customer invoice → primary document. The customer receives the documents a Ukrainian accountant expects, without printing and attaching them by hand.

The forms themselves come from l10n_ua_doc_reports (Рахунок-фактура, Акт виконаних робіт, Видаткова накладна). This module only decides which of them go into which e-mail.

Features

  • Order e-mails (quotation, order confirmation, payment executed) attach the Рахунок-фактура instead of the standard Odoo quotation / order PDF
  • Invoice e-mails attach the primary document next to the Odoo invoice PDF: service act, delivery note, or chosen per invoice from its lines (services only → act, goods → delivery note, both → both documents)
  • The Odoo invoice PDF and electronic invoicing keep working unchanged; the extra documents appear in the Send wizard and can be removed there before sending
  • Automatic invoicing after an online payment exposed in the same settings block: a paid web order is confirmed, invoiced, the invoice is posted and e-mailed with the act
  • Payments through a payment provider in test mode never create invoices, so no test income reaches the books; the order is still confirmed
  • Settings per company; the mail templates of Odoo are not modified, uninstalling the module restores the standard behaviour

Configuration

  1. Open Settings → Invoicing → Ukrainian Documents.
  2. Keep Рахунок-фактура in Order E-mails enabled (default).
  3. Choose the Primary Document in Invoice E-mails: service act (default), delivery note, by invoice content, or none.
  4. For an online shop enable Invoice Automatically after Online Payment and optionally choose the invoice e-mail template. The invoicing policy must be "Ordered quantities".
  5. Fill in the company requisites (ЄДРПОУ, legal address, bank account, director) used by the printed forms.

Requirements

  • Ukraine - Accounting Document Reports (l10n_ua_doc_reports), Sales, Invoicing
  • Any Odoo payment provider for online payments, e.g. Ukraine - LiqPay Payment Provider
  • Automatic invoicing is a setting of Odoo shared by all companies and websites of the database

Developed for Odoo 19 Community Edition.
